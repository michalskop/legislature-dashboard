# About nrsr.datatimes.cz

nrsr.datatimes.cz is a public dashboard for analysing activity and voting behaviour in the Slovak National Council (Národná rada SR).

The site uses public parliamentary data from nrsr.sk and derived analyses for attendance, rebelliousness, government alignment, and voting-position visualisation (WPCA).

Machine-readable discovery files are available at https://nrsr.datatimes.cz/llms.txt, https://nrsr.datatimes.cz/sitemap.xml, and https://nrsr.datatimes.cz/.well-known/agent-skills/index.json.
