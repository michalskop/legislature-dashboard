# Slovak National Council Datasets

## Overview

Use this skill to understand the main derived data categories used by the nrsr.datatimes.cz dashboard.

## Data Categories

### Attendance
- URL: https://nrsr.datatimes.cz/members
- Description: MP attendance shares across roll-call voting events.

### Rebelliousness
- URL: https://nrsr.datatimes.cz/members
- Description: Votes against the MP's own parliamentary group where comparable.

### Government Alignment
- URL: https://nrsr.datatimes.cz/groups
- Description: Agreement with the governing coalition.

### Voting Positions
- URL: https://nrsr.datatimes.cz/
- Description: WPCA voting-position analysis.
